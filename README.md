# PNG Passport Form FA 81 – Practice Copy

A fillable practice copy of the Papua New Guinea passport application form (Form FA 81).

**This is not an official form.** Use it to practise and check your answers, then copy them onto the real paper form and sign by hand.

## What it does
- Type into every box and tick every square, the same as the paper form
- Saves every change automatically in your browser (on this device)
- Keep several forms – one per person – and switch between them
- Start a new blank form any time
- Delete forms you no longer need
- Download a finished form as a 4-page A4 PDF
- Print

## How to use
1. Open the page.
2. Fill in the form. It saves as you go.
3. Tap **Download PDF** when you're done.
4. Copy the answers onto the official form and sign it by hand.

## Notes
- Everything stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Clearing your browser data will delete saved forms, so download a PDF of anything you want to keep.
- Signatures are never filled in. They must be done by hand on the official form.
